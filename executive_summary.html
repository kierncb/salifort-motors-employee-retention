Can we predict who leaves?

What HR can do

Cap the number of projects per employee.
Promote at four years, or find out why that group is unhappy.
Reward long hours, or stop requiring them.
Make overtime pay and time-off expectations explicit.
Hold team and company-wide talks on work culture.
Score evaluations on contribution, not just hours above 200 a month.

Next steps

Raise recall on leavers: class weights, thresholds, tree-based models.
Interview people near four years before changing promotion policy.
Re-run after a project cap to see if turnover at 6–7 projects drops.
